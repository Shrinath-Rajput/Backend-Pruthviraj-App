PRUTHVIRAJ Workforce & Business Management System - Complete Project Structure
================================================================================

backend/

│   │   │   ├── auth.validation.js   # Zod validation schemas for phone numbers, 6-digit OTP codes, and refresh tokens
│   │   │   ├── auth.routes.js       # Express router mounting /api/v1/auth public and protected endpoints
